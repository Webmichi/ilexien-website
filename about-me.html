Artist Statement

My goal is to bring joy, inspiration and whismy to my audience through my art. As an artist, having someone say to you "you inspire me" is the highest compliment you can recieve since we all have an artist that we admire so much. So, if I can be that for someone then I have truly made it as an artist. As for to type of art I make, I'm a bit multi-media. I do traditional and digital illustrations along with 2D animations. I primarily work with characters but I have been learn to do environments to better improve my art. Although, I also have experince working with various Adobe products but I am specify certified in Illustrator, Photoshop, and InDesign.
